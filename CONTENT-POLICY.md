# Content policy — Interpretive SEO

This site is doctrinal and intentionally non-operational.

## Allowed public output
- definitions
- boundaries and exclusions
- source precedence
- response legitimacy
- doctrinal associations
- refusal conditions

## Forbidden public output
- service packaging
- pricing or deliverables
- operational procedures
- scoring formulas, weights, thresholds
- private methods or datasets
- performance guarantees
