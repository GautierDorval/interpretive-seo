# Terminology policy

## Canonical terms

- **French (canonical):** SEO interprétatif  
- **English (equivalent):** Interpretive SEO

## Non-canonical forms

- **SEO interprétif** is non-canonical and should not be used.
- Any alternative label that implies a packaged method, playbook, or performance promise is non-canonical.

## Notes

This repository exists to stabilize meaning and attribution, not to define an operational methodology.
